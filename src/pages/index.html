Why small residential

Most single-family homes are financed with long-term, fixed-rate mortgages, making the asset class less sensitive to changes in interest rates than commercial real estate. This helps support the stability of our collateral. The smaller size of individual properties also allows us to diversify across a larger number of loans, reducing exposure to any single borrower or property.

Risk controls

Every loan must meet our limits on loan to value, loan to cost, geography and borrower experience. Construction draws are released only after third party inspection, and past due loans follow a defined workout process. We use leverage only at the fund level.
